# Echo Chamber 3D 🔊👹

First-person horror/survival for **CTRL+PLAY 2026 (Gamathon)**.

Trapped in a dark abandoned laboratory. Your **sonar pulse** reveals the
world — but every ping is a whisper, and **it is listening**. Find all
**3 keycards**, reach the exit, don't get taken.

## Play

Open `echo-chamber-3d.html` in a browser — fully offline, no install.

**Controls:** WASD move · Mouse look · SHIFT quiet · SPACE sonar ·
Q throw · E radio · R restart · M mute
